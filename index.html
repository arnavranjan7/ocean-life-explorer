Your ocean fact
✨

The ocean makes much of the oxygen we breathe.

Marine photosynthesizers, especially microscopic phytoplankton, contribute a large share of Earth's oxygen production. They are tiny, but their impact is planet-sized.

🧊

The ocean stores heat

Water absorbs and moves enormous amounts of heat, helping shape weather and climate around the world.

🌌

Many deep-sea animals make light

Bioluminescence can help animals attract prey, communicate, camouflage themselves or avoid predators.

Respect the power of the sea

Wonder is better with awareness.

The ocean is amazing, but some natural conditions can be dangerous. Understanding them helps people enjoy the coast more safely.

🌊

Rip currents

Fast-moving water can pull swimmers away from shore. Swim at patrolled beaches, heed flags and lifeguards, and never try to fight a current by swimming straight against it.

🪼

Stinging animals

Some jellyfish and other marine animals can sting. Do not touch unfamiliar sea life, and follow local beach advice about species and first aid.

🦈

Wildlife has space

Sharks and other large animals are part of healthy ecosystems. Keep a respectful distance, avoid feeding wildlife and follow local guidance.

⛈️

Storms & waves

Waves, sudden weather changes, cold water and slippery rocks can become hazardous quickly. Check forecasts and never turn your back on the sea.

Remember: Ocean safety advice varies by coast and conditions. Follow lifeguards and local authorities, and do not enter the water when warnings are active.
